Before you ask

A few things
people usually want to know.

Not sure where to start, what it costs, or how long it takes? Quick answers below.

Q · 01

How do you price a project?

No hourly charges — pricing is based on square feet and kitchen units. After a site visit we share a full proposal with quotes, materials, finishes, accessories and reference images.

Q · 02

What's the typical process?

Site visit and brief → complete case study → detailed proposal → design sign-off → workshop & installation → handover. You'll know what's coming at every step.

Q · 03

What happens after handover?

Our support continues after the project is complete. If something needs attention down the line, we're a call away — that's the Santana commitment.
