What we do

Six specialities. One studio.
Delivered turnkey, end to end.

01 · Kitchens

Modular Kitchens.

The kitchen is where a home actually lives. We design layouts that work for how you cook — not how someone else cooks — and finish them with quiet, hardworking materials.

  • Ergonomic layouts: L-shape, parallel, island, peninsula
  • German & Indian hardware: soft-close, push-to-open, lift-up
  • Premium counter materials: quartz, granite, sintered stone
  • Integrated appliance planning & service routing
  • 10-year warranty on carcass & hardware
Discuss your kitchen
02 · Storage

Wardrobes.

Custom-built wardrobes that solve real storage problems — sliding, hinged or walk-in — with internals planned around what you actually own.

  • Sliding, hinged & walk-in configurations
  • Mirror, lacquer, veneer, fluted & textured shutters
  • Soft-close drawers, pull-out trays, tie/belt organisers
  • Integrated LED lighting & charging points
  • Floor-to-ceiling planning for small Indian bedrooms
Plan your wardrobe
03 · Ceilings

False Ceilings.

Ceilings shape how a room feels. We use coves, drops and integrated lighting to add architectural calm — without making the space feel lower or busier.

  • POP, gypsum & mixed-material designs
  • Cove lighting & profile lighting integration
  • Speaker, AC grille & sensor planning built in
  • Crack-resistant detailing for Indian humidity
  • Designs sized for 9ft, 10ft & 12ft ceilings
Talk about ceilings
04 · Media

TV & Media Units.

Media walls that hide the cables, the consoles and the clutter — and showcase the few objects worth showing. Built to integrate with your sound, screen and storage.

  • Cable management & concealed power planning
  • Hidden storage for consoles, routers & accessories
  • Floating, full-wall & back-lit configurations
  • Stone, wood, metal & lacquer finish options
  • Pre-wiring for soundbars & surround systems
Design your media wall
05 · Entry

Foyer Designs.

The first three feet of your home set the tone for everything that follows. We design entries that make a first impression and quietly do the work — shoes, keys, mail, mirror.

  • Console, mirror & lighting compositions
  • Concealed shoe storage & key/wallet trays
  • Statement wall finishes: fluted wood, stone, art
  • Rangoli & deity niche planning where required
  • Vaastu-conscious layout options on request
Reimagine your entry
06 · Storage

Shoe Racks.

The everyday problem that ruins an otherwise beautiful entryway. We design shoe storage that disappears into the architecture — and actually fits everything you own.

  • Built-in, free-standing & bench-combo formats
  • Pull-out, tilt-down & soft-close mechanisms
  • Ventilated internals to prevent odour
  • Tall-boot, sneaker & sandal-specific zones
  • Seating, mirror & storage in one unit
Sort your entryway
