About Santana Interiors

End-to-end home design, delivered with care.

We offer complete solutions for your home — design and execution for bedrooms, living spaces, kitchens, or full-home transformations. Our team of expert designers and carpenters brings nearly 15 years of industry experience.

Pricing is transparent and straightforward: no hourly charges, only based on square feet and kitchen units. We ensure complete satisfaction and our support continues even after project completion.

After an initial discussion at your site, we conduct a complete case study and send a proposal with full details — quotes, requirements, materials, finishes, accessories, and images. So you know exactly what you're getting before any work begins.

What we believe

Six principles,
held lightly but firmly.

The values that quietly shape every drawing, every decision, every site visit.

01

Live with it.

Design for ten years, not ten months. Trends fade — proportions don't.

02

Edit ruthlessly.

The most considered spaces are the ones where everything earns its place.

03

Honour the material.

Solid wood looks like solid wood. Marble feels like marble. Nothing pretends.

04

Light is everything.

Natural by day, layered by night. Lighting plans matter as much as floor plans.

05

One team, one promise.

From design through delivery, a single accountable contact — no handoffs lost.

06

Done is sacred.

Fixed timelines. Honest budgets. Snag-free handovers. Always.
